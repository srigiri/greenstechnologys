Questions

Common questions about AI training.

Who should attend this AI course?

Professionals in analytics, data science, e-commerce and search, software professionals, and new graduates looking for a career change.

What are the prerequisites?

None. Anyone can take the course and grow into a machine learning or AI engineer, whatever their previous knowledge. It starts from Python basics.

What will I learn?

Deep learning basics, artificial neural networks, CNNs and RNNs, TensorFlow and Keras, supervised and unsupervised learning, machine learning using Python, image recognition, NLP and recommender systems.

What certification will I get?

Every learner receives an industry-recognised course completion certificate. The course also prepares you for applied data science with AI certification and the Azure AI Engineer Associate certification.

Will you help with placement?

Yes. We offer placement assistance with mock interviews, group discussions and soft skills support, and we arrange interviews with IT companies.

Is online training available?

Yes. Instructor-led online classes run at timings chosen around your availability. You can interact with the trainer, clarify doubts, submit assignments online and revisit recorded sessions.

Are AI jobs in demand?

AI skills are sought after by companies worldwide, and roles such as AI engineer, machine learning engineer and data scientist commonly ask for them.

Which languages are classes taught in?

Classes are available in English and Tamil.

Where is your centre?

Our centre is in Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Contact us for the address, directions and class timings.
