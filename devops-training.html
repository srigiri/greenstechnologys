Questions

Common questions about DevOps training.

Who can learn DevOps?

College freshers, working professionals switching careers, and experienced professionals upgrading their skills. You do not need to know every programming language.

What are the prerequisites?

No prior IT experience is required. Linux, shell scripting and Git are taught from scratch in Track 1.

Is DevOps worth learning?

Yes. DevOps joins software development and IT operations, and it is in steady demand across IT companies.

How long does the course take?

Plan for about one month of tools training and one month of placement preparation. Regular classes take around 60 days, and weekend classes take 11 to 12 weekends.

What will I get after the training?

A course completion certificate, plus preparation for CKA and CKAD, the Terraform Associate exam and, after an AWS Associate certification, the AWS DevOps Engineer Professional exam.

How do I know the course fees?

Call +91 89399 15577 for the current fee structure. You can attend a free demo first, and fees can be paid in two instalments.

Do you help with placement?

Yes. The placement team runs mock interviews, group discussions and soft-skill sessions, and keeps sending you for interviews until you get a job.

Is online training available?

Yes. Online timings are chosen based on your availability, and you can submit assignments online. Classes are in English and Tamil.
