Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect the details you share with us. Last updated: 14 October 2026.

1. Who we are

Greens Technology (“we”, “us”) runs software training courses at our Coimbatore centre and online. We decide why and how the personal data described here is used, which makes us the “Data Fiduciary” under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (the DPDP Act). You can reach us at contact@greenstechnologys.com or +91 89399 15577.

2. What we collect

When you submit an enquiry or “Request a callback” form, or request a course syllabus, we collect:

  • your phone number, and your name where the form asks for it;
  • the course you are interested in;
  • the page you submitted the form from, and the date and time;
  • a record that you ticked the consent box.

If you message us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp’s own terms apply to that conversation. This site stores your light or dark theme choice in your browser. If we add analytics or advertising tools, we will list them here.

3. Why we use it

We use these details only to contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about your enquiry or the syllabus you requested: to answer questions, share course details, batches and fees, and arrange a demo class. We ask for your consent before using them, using the tick box beside the form.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell your details. We share them only with service providers that help us run the website and handle enquiries, such as our hosting and email delivery providers, and only so they can provide that service to us.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your enquiry details only as long as needed for the purpose above. After that we delete them, unless the law requires us to keep them.

6. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we hold about you and how we use it;
  • ask us to correct or erase your data;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, as easily as you gave it (we will then stop contacting you and erase the data, unless the law requires us to keep it);
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights for you if you are unable to;
  • make a complaint to us, and if we do not resolve it, to the Data Protection Board of India.

To use any of these rights, email contact@greenstechnologys.com with your name and phone number.

7. Grievance contact

Grievance Officer, Greens Technology, Coimbatore, Tamil Nadu. Email: contact@greenstechnologys.com.

8. Children

Our enquiry forms are meant for people aged 18 and over. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian to submit the form for you, because we need a parent’s or guardian’s consent before using a child’s personal data.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your details from loss and unauthorised access. No method of sending data over the internet is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will post the new version on this page and update the date at the top.
